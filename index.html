The Complete Route

Seven Ports. One Unforgettable Voyage.

A carefully curated journey through the most celebrated cities and islands of the Mediterranean Sea.

The Mediterranean Route
Barcelona → Marseille → Genoa → Naples → Valletta → Santorini → Athens → Barcelona
Stop 01

Barcelona

Spain · Departure & Return

Begin in Barcelona, a vibrant Mediterranean city famous for its architecture, lively boulevards, coastal atmosphere, and exceptional cuisine.

Stop 02

Marseille

France

Discover the historic Old Port, the beauty of Provence, colorful waterfront streets, and the charm of southern France.

Stop 03

Genoa

Italy

Explore the historic Italian maritime city, its narrow old streets, grand architecture, palaces, and authentic Ligurian cuisine.

Stop 04

Naples

Italy

Experience the vibrant character of southern Italy, its celebrated pizza, historic neighborhoods, and rich cultural heritage.

Stop 05

Valletta

Malta

Discover golden limestone architecture, historic fortifications, charming streets, and beautiful harbor views.

Stop 06

Santorini

Greece

Admire whitewashed villages, blue-domed churches, dramatic volcanic cliffs, and breathtaking Aegean sunsets.

Stop 07

Athens

Greece · Piraeus Port

Explore the historic birthplace of democracy, the Acropolis, ancient Greek landmarks, and the culture of modern Athens.

Final Stop

Barcelona

Spain · Homecoming

Return to Barcelona and complete the Mediterranean round-trip journey, carrying memories that will last a lifetime.
